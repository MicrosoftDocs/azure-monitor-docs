> [!IMPORTANT]
> This article applies to Chaos Studio Classic: chaos experiments, targets and capabilities, and the Chaos Studio agent. Classic is the legacy model of Azure Chaos Studio. There's no further feature development on the classic model, and only critical fixes, such as security updates, are considered for backport. For new resilience testing, use [Chaos Studio Workspaces](../chaos-studio-workspaces-overview.md), where development is active. To understand the differences between the two models, see [Compare workspaces and experiments](../chaos-studio-workspaces-vs-experiments.md).
